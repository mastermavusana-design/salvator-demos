# Stock photo library: tyres & wheels (phase 1)

Free-licence photos found on Unsplash and Pexels (19 Oct 2026). Use them only to fill gaps when a shop has no usable photos of its own, always captioned "Your photo here" and credited "Some photos: Unsplash/Pexels" in the footer.

How to request a size:

- Unsplash: `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&q=80` (also give `srcset` at w=600 / 1000 / 1600)
- Pexels: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/<n>/pexels-photo-<n>.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1000` (same srcset widths)

Before first use, open the photo once and check: no chain-store signage, no big manufacturer logo filling the frame, no people who look like they're working unsafely, nothing that looks like a junk yard. If it fails, mark it ✗ here.
