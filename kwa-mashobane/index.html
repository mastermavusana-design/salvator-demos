Our story

Started in 2020, right here in Zondi.

Musa Khumalo, known to everyone as Mashobane, opened Kwa-Mashobane in 2020, in the middle of COVID-19, with a team of just five people. The plan was simple: honest kasi-style food, with traditional meals on the menu from time to time.

Today the kitchen serves breakfast and lunch, caters for business meetings, and has collected more than 2,000 ratings on Uber Eats.
