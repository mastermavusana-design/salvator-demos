How to read your tyre size

Every tyre has its size moulded on the sidewall. Here's what each part means. Tap a part to see.

195/65R1591H
  • 195Width of the tyre in millimetres, wall to wall.
  • 65Sidewall height as a % of the width. Lower means a thinner, sportier profile.
  • RRadial construction, which almost every car tyre is today.
  • 15Rim size in inches. It must match your wheel.
  • 91HLoad index (91 = 615 kg per tyre) and speed rating (H = up to 210 km/h).
